# Dashboard appearance

The dashboard has three independent visual designs: Studio Light, Mission Control and Bold Broadcast. Click the design button in the top bar (or the sparkle button in the sidebar) to cycle through them. For direct selection, open **Settings & About → General → Dashboard → Make it yours**.

Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** independently of the design. System tracks changes to the device preference. Both choices are saved locally in the browser. A new browser starts with Studio and System; previously saved light/dark/system modes are retained. The old glass selection migrates to Mission Control in dark mode.

The functional dashboard, its navigation, and its backend remain the same. On narrow or short screens the page scrolls and controls wrap rather than being compressed into a single screen.
